## 5. How to Update Google Business Profile (Under 10 Minutes)

1. Log in to Google Business Profile: https://business.google.com
2. Set Categories — Edit Profile > Business Category:
   - Primary: Commercial Printer
   - Secondary 1: Digital Printing Service
   - Secondary 2: Advertising Agency (or Banner Store)
   - Secondary 3: Corporate Gift Supplier
   - Secondary 4: Graphic Designer (for Logo Design, Urdu, Healthcare)
   - Remove any extra unrelated categories.
3. Add Services — Edit Profile > Services:
   - Under each category, add the Google Service Name and paste the GBP Description (300 chars max) from Section 3 above.
   - Add pricing where available.
4. Save — Changes reflect on Google Maps & Search within 24 hours.

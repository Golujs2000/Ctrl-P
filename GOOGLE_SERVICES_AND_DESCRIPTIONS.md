## 4. How to Update on Google Business Profile (Under 5 Minutes)

1. Log in to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com).
2. Go to **Edit Profile** > **Business category**:
   - Set **Primary category:** `Commercial Printer`
   - Set **3 Additional categories:**
     1. `Digital Printing Service`
     2. `Banner Store` *(or Advertising Agency)*
     3. `Corporate Gift Supplier`
   - Delete all other extra categories (Sign Shop, Custom T-Shirt Store, Invitation Printing Service).
   - Click **Save**.
3. Go to **Edit Profile** > **Services**:
   - You will now only see these 4 clear categories.
   - Simply copy and paste the **Google Service Name** and **GBP Description (≤300 chars)** from Section 3 above into each category.
4. Changes will reflect cleanly on Google Maps and Search.
